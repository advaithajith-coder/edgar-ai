## Suggested story
1. **Problem:** a 10-K is 100+ pages; analysts need numbers *and* reasons, and need to trust both. (01)
2. **Instant view, no AI:** pick a company, key figures and statements straight from SEC EDGAR. (03)
3. **Ask anything:** EDGAR.AI plans, calls tools, and answers with tables and charts. (04b, 04)
4. **Why, not just what:** it reads the MD&A and quotes management, with sources. (05, 05b, 06)
5. **Trustworthy:** it says what it can't answer; every answer shows how it was calculated. (07, 02)

Note: the AI answers (shots 04 to 07) are live model outputs, so wording varies run to run.
